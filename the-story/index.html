Behind the Corner: From Code to Craft

Hi there. I'm Yolinda.

For over 15 years, my professional life has involved staring at glowing screens, architecting systems, and analyzing mountains of data as a Developer and Data Analyst. I love my career, but let's be honest - 'n mens se brein kan net soveel data hanteer voordat jy heeltemal mal word (a person's brain can only handle so much data before you go completely crazy). Eventually, I just needed a way to step away from the keyboard, quiet the analytical noise, and make something physical that I could actually hold in my hands.

I'm a natural introvert, but I have this relentless, optimistic energy when it comes to learning how things work. Over the years, I've tried my hand at almost everything. Pouring glossy epoxy resin art? Loved it. Building a custom wood and cinderblock bookshelf just because an AI TikTok video made it look easy? Challenge accepted and conquered. Crocheting? Turns out, I'm pretty great at it.

But my curious nature also means I've learned what I absolutely despise. I once tried my hand at knitting and sewing, and nee dankie - those hobbies were swiftly banished to the "never again" pile.

Curiosity Corner was born out of this exact experimental chaos.

Curiosity Corner was born out of this exact experimental chaos. I wanted a dedicated, personal space online to share my interests, post about the local events I enjoy, share some recipes I love cooking, and—most importantly - sell the things I genuinely love making.

Because I strongly prefer to stay happily behind the scenes and off-camera, this brand isn't about my face - it's entirely about the craft. So, what can you expect to find in the shop?

Honestly, whatever I am hyper-fixated on creating at any given moment. I don't do rigid corporate categories, boring product niches, or five-year inventory plans. If I try a new creative skill, fall in love with the process, and make something high-quality, I will put it up here for you to buy. If my curiosity leads me to a completely different medium next month? Well, dan speel ons met iets nuuts (then we play with something new).

It is a shop driven purely by curiosity, trial, error, and handmade craft.

Life is too short to do only one thing. Whether you're here to grab something unique I've made, find a new recipe, or just see what crazy DIY project I'm tackling next, I'm glad you stopped by.

Welkom by die hoekie.
